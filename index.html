FAQ

Good to know before you come

Do I need an appointment?+
No — walk in anytime during opening hours. If you want zero waiting, call ahead with your tyre size and we'll have it ready when you arrive.
What tyre sizes do you carry?+
All common car, SUV and 4×4 sizes from 12" to 24", including performance, run-flat and all-terrain options from every major brand.
How long does fitting take?+
Most cars are done in about 15 minutes — that includes mounting, balancing and a new valve. Full sets and alignment take a little longer.
Do you offer wheel alignment and balancing?+
Yes. We run computerised 3D alignment and wheel balancing to remove vibration, protect your new tyres and keep the steering straight.
Where exactly are you located?+
Ras Al Khor Industrial Area 2, Dubai (Plus code 59H9+R9). Easy access with parking — see the map below for directions.
